## For Employers

MeritPoint Pathways supports employers by helping connect roles with motivated candidates—especially those who are upskilling, switching careers, or re-entering the workforce.

Our approach focuses on **role fit**, **readiness**, and **clear expectations**—so hiring is smoother for both employers and candidates.

---

## What we can support

### Candidate referrals and shortlisting
We can help identify candidates based on:
- Skills and prior experience
- Training background and job readiness
- Career direction and role alignment

### Role clarity support (optional)
If needed, we can help refine:
- What the role requires (must-haves vs nice-to-haves)
- What training or onboarding may be required
- What a realistic candidate profile looks like

### Community outreach and partnerships
We aim to build connections through:
- Workshops and information sessions
- Partnerships with training providers and community organizations
- Outreach to job seekers exploring in-demand pathways

---

## Best-fit roles

We’re particularly suited to support hiring pathways connected to:
- Skilled Trades
- Healthcare support roles
- Entry-to-mid level roles where training and progression matter

(We can still support other roles depending on requirements.)

## How to get started

Send us:
- Role title and location
- Key requirements and schedule
- Preferred start date
- Any screening questions you want candidates to answer

Go to the [Contact page]({{ "/contact/" | relative_url }}) and choose **“Employer / Hiring Support”** in your message.
