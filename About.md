## About MeritPoint Pathways

MeritPoint Pathways is an education and career guidance platform built to simplify complex decisions—especially when you’re choosing a program, exploring a new career, or trying to understand funding options.

We support:
- **Students** choosing the right program
- **Working professionals** planning career growth or switching fields
- **Newcomers** and internationally educated individuals navigating Canadian pathways
- **Older adults** returning to the workforce or upskilling

Our focus is practical: clear steps, realistic timelines, and honest guidance.

---

## What we stand for

### Clarity over confusion
We break down requirements and “next steps” into simple, usable plans.

### Client-focused guidance
We don’t push one-size-fits-all solutions. We help you choose what fits your goals, background, and timeline.

### Trust and transparency
Funding and eligibility vary by person and program. We explain what applies to your situation and what documents you’ll need.
