## Funding & Eligibility Guidance

Funding rules can feel confusing—especially when different programs, institutions, and life situations have different requirements. MeritPoint Pathways helps you understand what may apply to you and what you need to prepare **before** you commit to a program.

> Important: Funding and coverage vary by person and by program. We provide guidance and planning support based on your situation.

---

## OSAP (Ontario Student Assistance Program)

OSAP may help eligible learners with education-related costs such as:
- Tuition (full or partial)
- Books and required supplies
- Program-related fees (where applicable)

In some cases, additional support may also be available depending on eligibility and circumstances.

### How we help with OSAP planning
- Explain the overall OSAP process in simple steps
- Create a document checklist and timeline
- Help you understand common terminology and requirements
- Connect your program choice with funding planning

## Upskilling and career training supports (varies by person)

Depending on your background, employment situation, and training goal, other supports may also exist for short-term training or workforce re-entry.

We help you:
- Identify realistic options based on your goal and timeline
- Understand what documentation may be required
- Plan next steps (without guessing)

---

## Before you apply: quick checklist

To speed things up, it helps to know:
- Your goal career or program
- Your current status (student / working / unemployed / newcomer / returning to work)
- Your location (province/city)
- Your timeline (when you want to start)
- Any previous education or credentials
