## What we do

MeritPoint Pathways provides step-by-step education and career guidance for **students**, **working professionals**, **career switchers**, and **older adults**. Our goal is simple: help you make confident decisions with a clear plan, realistic timelines, and next steps you can actually follow.

**Free consultation • No upfront fees**

---

## 1) Program & Career Planning

**Best for:** Students choosing a program, career changers, and newcomers planning their first Canadian career step.

We help you:
- Shortlist programs that match your goals, background, and timeline
- Understand prerequisites and what to do next
- Build a practical career progression plan (not generic advice)

---

## 2) Funding Guidance (OSAP & Other Options)

**Best for:** Anyone trying to understand what support may be available before committing to a program.

We help you:
- Understand how funding *may* cover costs like **tuition**, **books/supplies**, and other required fees (eligibility varies)
- Plan your application timeline and documentation checklist
- Navigate options such as **OSAP** and other training support programs (where applicable)

> Note: Funding coverage varies by person and program. We focus on clarity and next steps based on your situation.

---

## 3) Career Pathways Library (Step-by-Step Guides)

**Best for:** Anyone who wants a roadmap for a specific career.

Our site includes structured guides across:
- **Skilled Trades** (HVAC, Electrician, Welder, Plumber, 310T, and more)
- **Healthcare** (PSW, Nursing pathways, etc.)
- **STEM & Professional Designations** (CPA and other designation/licensing routes)

Each pathway explains:
- Entry requirements and prerequisites
- Multiple routes (where applicable)
- Steps from start to finish
- Links to official sources for verification

---

## 4) Job Search & Career Readiness

**Best for:** Job seekers who need direction and structure.

We support:
- Role targeting based on skills and experience
- Resume/LinkedIn guidance and interview preparation direction
- A clear plan to move from training to employment

---

## 5) For Employers (Talent Matching)

**Best for:** Employers looking for motivated, job-ready candidates.

We can help with:
- Candidate shortlisting based on skill fit
- Community outreach and referrals
- Aligning expectations and readiness for better hiring outcomes
