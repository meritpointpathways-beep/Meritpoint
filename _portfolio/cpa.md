## Overview
The CPA designation is earned through a structured process that combines:
- prerequisite learning (specific subject areas)
- the CPA Professional Education Program (CPA PEP)
- the Common Final Examination (CFE)
- required practical experience

This guide outlines **two common pathways**, then a clear step-by-step plan.

---

## Pathway 1 — You already have a degree that meets prerequisites
**Best for:** People with an undergraduate degree that already includes the required prerequisite coverage.

### Steps
1) **Confirm prerequisites**
   - Check if your completed courses meet CPA prerequisite requirements.

2) **Apply to CPA Ontario as a student**
   - Registration opens access to the student journey tools and guidance.

3) **Complete CPA PEP**
   - Work through the required modules and program structure.

4) **Write the CFE**
   - Final exam step after completing the required education components.

5) **Complete practical experience**
   - Record and report experience as required while developing competencies.

---

## Pathway 2 — You need missing prerequisites (bridging / prep)
**Best for:** Career changers, international backgrounds, or degrees missing required subject areas.

### Steps
1) **Identify missing prerequisite subjects**
   - Review what’s missing vs. CPA requirements.

2) **Complete prerequisite/preparatory courses**
   - Use approved options (CPA preparatory courses or recognized equivalents).

3) **Enter CPA PEP**
   - Once prerequisite learning is complete.

4) **CFE + Practical Experience**
   - Same completion requirements apply.

---

## Practical Experience (PE) — What to expect
- Practical experience is a required component, reported through CPA’s reporting tools.
- Many students complete practical experience while doing education modules.

---

## Notes (important)
- Requirements and program structures can change over time.
- Ontario-specific guidance may differ from other provinces.
